📖 Timesheet Management Guide

Welcome! Your primary goal as the Timekeeper is to make sure every worker is assigned to the correct supervisor, all numbers match, and the official Timesheets are printed and ready every Friday afternoon for the upcoming week.

Step 1: Log in to Your Profile

  • In the top right corner, find the Profil drop-down menu.
  • Select ⏱️ Timekeeper and enter your password. This unlocks your ability to move workers and print documents.

Step 2: Clear the "Unassigned" List

  • Click the floating 👷 Base Ouvriers button at the bottom right.
  • Look at the "Ouvriers Non Assignés" box. If there are names here, they are not on any Timesheet.
  • Click on several workers (or drag them simultaneously) to assign them directly into the Supervisor's box.

Step 3: Check for Red Warnings ⚠️

  • On every task, there is a counter: 👥 12 / 12 (Actual names assigned / Manual planned number).
  • If a box is RED, you have a mismatch (too many people, or not enough).
  • Find the missing workers and assign them into the correct box.
  • If you cannot fix it: Alert the Cost Control Team immediately so they can investigate.

Step 4: The Friday Printing Routine

  • Check the small square box (☑) for every Supervisor who needs a Timesheet.
  • Click the black 📄 Imprimer TS button.
  • Select the date of the next Monday.
  • The system will automatically generate 6 pages per team (Monday to Saturday), properly paginated (max 15 workers per page), with signature blocks on every page.

📖 Timesheet Management Guide

Welcome! Your primary goal as the Timekeeper is to make sure every worker is assigned to the correct supervisor, all numbers match, and the official Timesheets are printed and ready every Friday afternoon for the upcoming week.

Step 1: Log in to Your Profile

  • In the top right corner, find the Profil drop-down menu.
  • Select ⏱️ Timekeeper and enter your password. This unlocks your ability to move workers and print documents.

Step 2: Clear the "Unassigned" List

  • Click the floating 👷 Base Ouvriers button at the bottom right.
  • Look at the "Ouvriers Non Assignés" box. If there are names here, they are not on any Timesheet.
  • Click on several workers (or drag them simultaneously) to assign them directly into the Supervisor's box.

Step 3: Check for Red Warnings ⚠️

  • On every task, there is a counter: 👥 12 / 12 (Actual names assigned / Manual planned number).
  • If a box is RED, you have a mismatch (too many people, or not enough).
  • Find the missing workers and assign them into the correct box.
  • If you cannot fix it: Alert the Cost Control Team immediately so they can investigate.

Step 4: The Friday Printing Routine

  • Check the small square box (☑) for every Supervisor who needs a Timesheet.
  • Click the black 📄 Imprimer TS button.
  • Select the date of the next Monday.
  • The system will automatically generate 6 pages per team (Monday to Saturday), properly paginated (max 15 workers per page), with signature blocks on every page.
